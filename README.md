# 🌐 Digital Village Connect
### Smart Rural Digital Assistance Portal

**Community Service Project (CSP)**

Digital Village Connect is a web-based platform developed as part of our Community Service Project titled **"Internet Usage in Villages"**. The main objective is to improve digital awareness among rural communities by providing simple information about Internet usage, agriculture, government services, and cyber safety in one place.

🌍 **Live Website:** https://tsanthosh982005.github.io/digital-village-connect/

---

## 📌 About the Project

The Internet plays an important role in education, communication, banking, agriculture, and government services. However, many rural people are not fully aware of the available digital resources and safe Internet practices.

To address this, we developed Digital Village Connect to provide easy access to useful digital information and online resources. The platform is designed with a simple interface to help users understand and access digital services.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Study Internet usage and digital awareness among villagers.
- Promote safe and responsible Internet usage.
- Provide information about useful government online services.
- Help farmers access agricultural information and resources.
- Create awareness about cyber threats and online fraud.
- Encourage digital literacy among different age groups.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🏠 Home:** Introduction to the Digital Village Connect platform.
- **🌐 Internet Awareness:** Information about the Internet, networks, protocols, email, and cloud computing.
- **🌾 Farmers:** Agricultural information, weather resources, market information, and government schemes.
- **🏛️ Government Services:** Useful links and guidance for Aadhaar, PAN, voter services, and other government portals.
- **🛡️ Cyber Safety:** Awareness about strong passwords, OTP protection, phishing, and online security.
- **📊 Survey Data Visualization:** Bar charts and pie charts representing observed village survey findings.
- **🌐 Language Support:** Language selection for English, Telugu, Hindi, and Tamil.
- **🔍 Search:** Find relevant topics across the website.
- **🌙 Dark Mode:** Comfortable viewing experience.
- **🖼️ Gallery:** Photographs of project activities and awareness programmes.
- **📱 Responsive Design:** Designed for desktop and mobile screens.
- **🕒 Digital Clock:** Displays the current time.

## 📊 Village Survey Findings

### Digital Awareness Among Villagers

| Category | Awareness |
|---|---:|
| Students | 90% |
| Youth | 85% |
| Farmers | 65% |
| Homemakers | 60% |
| Elderly | 40% |

### Purpose of Internet Usage

| Purpose | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Communication | 35% |
| Education | 25% |
| Entertainment | 20% |
| Social Media | 15% |
| Digital Payments | 5% |

**Key Finding:** Communication was the primary purpose of Internet usage, followed by education. The survey observations also highlighted the need to improve digital awareness among elderly people and other less digitally experienced groups.

*Note: These percentages represent the observed survey data collected for this project.*
