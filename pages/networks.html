📡 Types of Networks

Learn how computers connect with each other through different types of networks.

Introduction

A computer network is a group of computers and devices connected together to share information and resources. Networks help people communicate quickly and access data from different locations.

🌐 Main Types of Networks

🏠

LAN

Local Area Network connects computers inside a home, office or school.

🏙

MAN

Metropolitan Area Network connects different places within a city.

🌍

WAN

Wide Area Network connects computers across countries and continents.

📊 Network Comparison

Network Coverage Example
LAN Building School Computer Lab
MAN City City Cable Network
WAN Country / World Internet

🌍 Real-Life Examples

🏫

School

School computers connected together form a LAN.

🏢

Office

Office departments share files through a LAN.

🌐

Internet

The Internet is the world's largest WAN.

💡 Did You Know?

The Internet itself is a huge WAN that connects billions of devices worldwide.

🎯 Summary

LAN is used for small areas, MAN connects an entire city, and WAN connects countries and the whole world. The Internet is the best example of a WAN.
