📧 Email

Learn how to create, send and receive emails safely.

Introduction

Email (Electronic Mail) is a digital method of sending and receiving messages through the Internet. It is one of the fastest and most widely used communication tools in the world. People use email for education, business, banking, government services and personal communication.

📬 Parts of an Email Address

@ → Separator

gmail.com → Email Provider

📋 Steps to Send an Email

Step Description
1 Open Gmail or any Email service.
2 Click Compose.
3 Enter the receiver's Email address.
4 Write the Subject.
5 Type your message.
6 Attach files if needed.
7 Click Send.

🔒 Email Safety Tips

🔑

Strong Password

Use a strong password and never share it.

🚫

Spam Emails

Avoid opening emails from unknown senders.

🔗

Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links or attachments.

✅

Verify Sender

Check the sender's email before replying.

💡 Did You Know?

More than 350 billion emails are sent worldwide every day for communication, education and business.

🎯 Summary

Email is a fast, secure and reliable way to communicate over the Internet. It helps people send messages, share documents, receive important notifications and access online services. Using strong passwords and avoiding suspicious emails helps keep your account safe.
