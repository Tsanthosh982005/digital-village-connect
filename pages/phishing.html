🎣 Phishing Scams

Learn how scammers trick people online and how to protect yourself.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is an online fraud where criminals pretend to be banks, government offices or trusted companies. Their goal is to steal your passwords, OTPs, bank details or personal information.

Example:

You receive a message saying: "Your bank account will be blocked. Click here immediately." This is usually a fake message designed to steal your information.

Common Types of Phishing

📧

Email Scams

Fake emails asking you to verify your account or password.

📱

SMS Scams

Fraudulent text messages containing fake links.

💬

WhatsApp Scams

Fake lottery, prize or emergency messages from unknown people.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites that look genuine but steal your login details.

Warning Signs

⚠

Urgent Messages

"Act now" or "Your account will be closed."

🔗

Unknown Links

Links from unknown senders should not be opened.

💳

Requests for OTP

Banks never ask for OTP or passwords through calls or messages.

❌

Spelling Mistakes

Many fake messages contain grammar or spelling errors.

How to Stay Safe

🔒

Never Share OTP

Keep your OTP, PIN and passwords secret.

🌐

Check Website

Always verify the website address before entering information.

☎

Contact Official Support

If unsure, call the official customer care number.

🚫

Ignore Suspicious Messages

Delete fake messages without clicking any links.

🎯 Summary

Phishing scams try to steal your personal information using fake messages, emails and websites. Never click unknown links or share your OTP or passwords. Always verify before trusting any online message.
