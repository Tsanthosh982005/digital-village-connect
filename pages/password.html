🔑 Strong Passwords

Learn how to create strong passwords to protect your online accounts and personal information.

Introduction

A password is the first layer of security for your online accounts. A strong password protects your email, bank account, social media and personal information from hackers.

What Makes a Strong Password?

🔠

Uppercase Letters

Include capital letters like A, B, C.

🔡

Lowercase Letters

Use small letters like a, b, c.

🔢

Numbers

Add numbers such as 1, 2, 3.

🔣

Special Symbols

Use symbols like @, #, $, %, &.

Examples of Passwords

❌ Weak Password ✅ Strong Password
123456 Village@2026#
password Farmer#Safe25
abc123 Digital@Village1
qwerty India@Secure99

Tips for Creating Strong Passwords

  • Use at least 8–12 characters.
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Include numbers and symbols.
  • Avoid using your name or birthday.
  • Use different passwords for different accounts.
  • Change passwords regularly.

💡 Remember

A strong password is like the lock on your house. The stronger the lock, the safer your valuables are.
