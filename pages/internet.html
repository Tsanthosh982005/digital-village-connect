🌐 What is the Internet?

Learn the basic concept of the Internet in simple language.

Introduction

The Internet is a worldwide network that connects millions of computers, smartphones and other digital devices. It allows people to communicate, share information, watch videos, learn online and access many useful services from anywhere in the world.

How does the Internet work?

  • Devices connect using Wi-Fi or mobile networks.
  • Information travels as small packets of data.
  • Routers direct the data to its destination.
  • Servers store websites and online information.
  • Your browser displays the requested webpage.

📱 Your Device ⬇ 📡 Wi-Fi / Mobile Data ⬇ 🌍 Internet ⬇ 🖥 Website Server ⬇ 📄 Webpage Displayed

🔒 Internet Safety Tips

🔑

Strong Password

Create unique passwords for every account.

📱

Never Share OTP

Keep your OTP confidential.

🌐

Trusted Websites

Always use HTTPS websites.

🛡

Antivirus

Keep antivirus software updated.

💡 Remember

Never share your OTP, password or ATM PIN with anyone. Always verify websites before entering personal information. Use only trusted websites and stay alert against online scams.
