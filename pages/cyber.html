🔒 Cyber Safety

Stay safe while using the Internet by protecting your personal information and avoiding online scams.

Introduction

Cyber Safety means using the Internet safely and protecting yourself from online fraud, hackers, fake websites and cyber criminals. Following simple safety rules can keep your personal information and money secure.

🛡 Common Online Threats

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails or websites that steal your passwords and personal information.

📱

OTP Fraud

Scammers ask for your OTP to steal money from your bank account.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that damages your device or steals your data.

💳

Online Banking Scam

Fraudsters pretend to be bank officials and ask for account details.

🔒 Cyber Safety Tips

🔑

Strong Password

Use long passwords with letters, numbers and symbols.

📲

Never Share OTP

Banks and government offices never ask for your OTP.

🌐

Use HTTPS Websites

Always check for the padlock icon before entering personal details.

📥

Download Carefully

Install apps only from trusted sources like Google Play Store or App Store.

🔄

Keep Software Updated

Regular updates fix security problems and protect your device.

🛡

Use Antivirus

Antivirus software helps detect and remove harmful programs.

🚨 What To Do If You Receive a Suspicious Message?

Situation What You Should Do
Unknown SMS Do not click any links.
OTP Request Never share your OTP.
Bank Call Verify using the official bank number.
Lottery Message Ignore and delete it.
Fake Website Close the website immediately.

📱 Safe Digital Habits

💳

Digital Payments

Use trusted apps like PhonePe, Google Pay or BHIM carefully.

👤

Privacy

Never share Aadhaar, PAN or bank details with strangers.

📶

Public Wi-Fi

Avoid banking or shopping while using public Wi-Fi.

📂

Regular Backup

Keep copies of important files in cloud storage or external drives.

📞 Cyber Crime Helpline

If you become a victim of online fraud, immediately report it by calling the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or visit the official Cyber Crime Portal at https://cybercrime.gov.in.

💡 Remember

✔ Never share your Password, OTP or ATM PIN.

✔ Always verify websites before entering personal information.

✔ Think before clicking unknown links.

✔ Stay alert to stay safe online.

🎯 Summary

Cyber Safety is essential for everyone using the Internet. By creating strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, protecting personal information and reporting cyber crimes quickly, you can use digital services safely and confidently.
