📱 OTP Safety

Learn how to protect your One-Time Password (OTP) and stay safe from online fraud.

What is an OTP?

OTP (One-Time Password) is a temporary security code sent to your registered mobile number or email. It is used to verify your identity while logging in, making online payments or changing account settings.

Example:

Your bank sends an OTP when you make an online payment. Enter the OTP only on the official website or app to complete the transaction.

Where is OTP Used?

🏦

Internet Banking

OTP is required to authorize online banking transactions.

💳

UPI Payments

Used for secure money transfers and online purchases.

📧

Email Login

Helps verify your identity when signing in.

🔒

Password Reset

OTP is used to securely reset forgotten passwords.

OTP Safety Rules

❌

Never Share OTP

Do not share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

📞

Beware of Fake Calls

Fraudsters may ask for your OTP pretending to help you.

📱

Read SMS Carefully

Check who sent the OTP before entering it.

👤

Use OTP Yourself

Enter the OTP only on trusted websites or official mobile apps.

Common OTP Frauds

  • Fake bank customer care calls.
  • Lottery and prize-winning scams.
  • KYC update fraud messages.
  • Fake online shopping refunds.
  • Unknown payment requests.

💡 Remember

Your OTP is like the key to your bank account. Never share it with anyone. Banks and government departments never ask for your OTP over the phone.
