☁ Cloud Computing

Learn how cloud technology stores and shares your files safely over the Internet.

Introduction

Cloud Computing is a technology that allows users to store files, photos, videos and documents on the Internet instead of only on their computer or mobile phone. This means you can access your files anytime and from any device connected to the Internet.

💾

🤝

Share documents and photos with family or friends instantly.

🔒 Cloud Safety Tips

🔑

Strong Password

Protect your cloud account with a strong password.

📲

Two-Factor Authentication

Enable OTP verification for better security.

🚫

Avoid Public Wi-Fi

Do not upload important files using unsafe networks.

🔄

Regular Backup

Keep important documents updated in the cloud.

💡 Did You Know?

Millions of people use cloud services every day to store photos, school documents, office files and important certificates securely.

🎯 Summary

Cloud Computing allows users to store and access files through the Internet instead of only on their devices. It provides safe storage, easy sharing, automatic backup and access from anywhere. Using strong passwords and secure Internet connections keeps cloud data protected.
