🔒 Cyber Safety

Stay safe while using the Internet and protect your personal information.

🔑 Strong Passwords

Create strong passwords using uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special symbols to keep your accounts secure.

📱 OTP Safety

Never share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank or any government office.

🎣 Phishing Scams

Avoid clicking unknown links or downloading suspicious files. Always verify websites before entering personal information.

Report lost mobile phones, check mobile numbers linked with your Aadhaar and protect yourself from telecom fraud.
